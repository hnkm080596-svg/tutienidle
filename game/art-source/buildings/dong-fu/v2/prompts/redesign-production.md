# Maintained ink-landscape building redesign

All six extraction masters use the approved spring-morning Dong Fu background and the inhabited Ký Bảo Các scene as style authorities. Each subject is front-facing with almost no side plane, simplified to landscape-painting detail density, and rendered on perfectly uniform `#FFFFFF` for mechanical white unmatting.

Shared constraints: same warm xuan-paper palette and broken dry-brush contours as the background; old but actively maintained; clean approach; minimal moss; no people, text, UI, hard sticker outline, oval shadow, pedestal, diorama, saturated fantasy glow, purple, magenta, checkerboard, or watermark.

- `spirit_spring`: low natural spring, short spillway, thin shelter, practical water vessel, and three restrained pale-jade stones.
- `equipment_hall`: low asymmetric working forge, cared-for roof, short chimney, recessed furnace, anvil, and sparse tool racks.
- `pill_room`: compact herbal workshop, soft octagonal suggestion, round windows, low furnace, herb racks, and orderly jars.
- `teleport_array`: maintained ancient stone gate, genuinely empty center, restrained geometric carving, and no portal glow or platform.
- `gathering_outpost`: low wide coordination hall with map table, rolled routes, spirit-wood bundles, ore baskets, and storage rack.
- `vendor`: Ký Bảo Các, an open-front sect exchange pavilion with treasure cabinet, bronze balance, chests, ledgers, and two subtle warm lamps.
