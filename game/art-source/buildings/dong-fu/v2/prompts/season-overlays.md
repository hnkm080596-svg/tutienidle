# Shared season overlay prompts

All four overlays were generated as sparse scene-space atmosphere plates on a uniform chroma-magenta extraction background, then keyed to genuine alpha and normalized to `1672 × 941`. The center cultivation platform remains clear. No building, mountain, UI, character, text, watermark, or paper rectangle is present.

- Spring: pale young bamboo and grass at the far lower corners, restrained pale blossoms near side edges, and faint damp mist marks.
- Summer: fuller green bamboo clusters at the far lower corners and a few isolated side leaves. The generated upper haze was removed during QA because it read as heavy gray blobs after extraction.
- Autumn: sparse ochre, muted-gold, and restrained cinnabar leaves with subtle dry-brush specks at outer edges.
- Winter: sparse low foreground snow drifts, a few static flakes, and no roof-specific snow.

Generated source plates are stored under `art-source/buildings/dong-fu/v2/shared/seasons-chroma/`; runtime textures are under `public/assets/buildings/dong-fu/v2/shared/seasons/`.
