## Reusable visual families

| Family | Reuse | Composition that must remain specific |
|---|---|---|
| Continuous paper page | `common/PcPaperScene.vue`: title/subtitle, optional navigation, tabs/default workspace/footer slots; `PcPaperTabs.vue`: items/selected/label → select(id); `PcPaperButton.vue`: primary/secondary/icon → click(event); `assets/pc-paper-scene.css`: slot/inspector recipes | Collection grid, body diagram, equipment sockets, graph, map, ladder, list and settings form are different workspaces. Shared shell does not supply topology, filters or commands. |
| Landscape opening/home | Warm world artwork, brush icon family, shared button/type/color vocabulary | Opening logo/CTA and home HUD/building hit targets require their own geometry; no full paper shell over the landscape. Auth/creation have a compact paper form over the world. |
| Compact popover/dialog | Paper/ink/gold vocabulary, slot visuals and buttons, existing Tooltip/OverlayPanel/ConfirmModal hosts | Content-sized chrome, anchor placement, modal focus/close semantics; never the 1440×810 page shell. |
| Battle/tribulation | Ink plates, icons, typography; compact detail/modal family for overlays | Battlefield, measured insets, order/vitals/action HUD, tribulation question/review regions. Never a paper graph workspace. |
| Outcome ceremony | Shared paper/art/button vocabulary | Reward/result hierarchy and footer commands, independent of collection navigation. Defeat recovery and victory rewards differ. |

Current prototype primitives have no stores/domain imports. Slot and inspector are CSS recipes, not typed data components. `PcBodyMeridianOverlay.vue` is decorative diagram presentation. `presentation/assets/PcPaperIcons.ts` owns icon URLs. Consumers must retain current pending, eligibility, confirmation, tooltip, audio, focus and feedback contracts when using visual primitives; the new buttons/tabs alone do not implement them.
