## Verdict

**FAIL — 0 Critical / 0 High / 1 Medium / 1 Low / 2 Nit.**

Every headline r34 claim verifies on the boot seam: the discard runs exactly once, strictly before `markReady`, on both arms that reach admission (ok-restore and the create/uninitialized grant funnel); generation-fence `skipped` arms intentionally do not pay it (frozen zombie kept — r33 doctrine, probed). The teardown is complete on every *observable* binding and provably silent — zero `eventBus.emit` during discard, so the `canMutate`-ordering concern is vacuous. The tail catch lands on a stopped clock, making the re-latch a no-op — deny direction either way. `discardFailedCycle` is the verbatim former body moved into a shared private — identical observable behavior.

The Medium is an **uncovered sibling seam of r34-COR-F1**, not a defect in the fix itself: `OnlineSessionController.attemptReconnect` → `App.vue` `onResume` `('replaced')` → `restoreGameSession` + `lifecycle.resumeSimulation()` performs the same in-place `$state` replacement the boot discard was built for, but runs **no battle discard** — a mid-pause remote head move resurrects the pre-pause battle as a ghost resolving into the restored character. Probed end-to-end on the real controller + real `restoreGameSession` + real store: the ghost survives, returns to `running`, and banks spirit stones into char B's material bag.
